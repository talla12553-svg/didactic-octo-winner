Our story

One grill, one family, since day one

Sene started as a single charcoal grill run by the Sene family, cooking the marinated chicken they grew up on for anyone walking past at lunchtime. The queue got long enough that a counter, then a kitchen, then a proper dining room followed.

The cooking has not changed. Chicken still marinates overnight in garlic, mustard and lemon. Potatoes are still cut by hand each morning. The sauces are still mixed in the back, in the same proportions, by people who will tell you straight if you order the mild one.

Fast food, in our reading, means you do not wait long — not that somebody cut a corner on the way there.
