Questions

Things people ask at the counter

Do you take card?

Card and contactless, yes. Cash too. Card is quicker at the lunch rush.

Can I book a table?

For groups of six or more, yes — use the form and pick “Eat in”. Smaller groups walk in.

Is there parking?

Street parking on Market Street and a public car park two minutes' walk away.

Halal?

All our chicken and beef is halal certified. Certificates are on the wall by the counter.

Do you cater events?

We do platters up to about forty people. Email us with the date and headcount.

Any vegan options?

The veggie rice bowl and hand-cut fries are vegan. The garden bean burger is vegetarian.
