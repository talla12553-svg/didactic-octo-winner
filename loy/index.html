Before you come in.

What's the difference between Parfum, EDP and EDT?

It's the concentration of oils. Parfum is the richest and lasts longest. Eau de parfum (EDP) is the everyday favorite, and eau de toilette (EDT) is lighter.

Can I try a scent before buying?

Yes. Every fragrance on the shelf can be tried at the counter.

Can I reserve a bottle?

Yes. Call us and we'll set it aside for you.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. Email or call us for the wholesale price list.

Why are you closed Friday mornings?

On Fridays we open at 5:00 PM and stay open until 9:30 PM.
